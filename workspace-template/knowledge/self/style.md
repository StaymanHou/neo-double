# How he writes

## What this was built from
Per channel: how many messages, which date range, what was excluded and why (forwarded
items, bot relays, previews truncated by the source, messages he says were AI-drafted).

---

## The through-line
What is true across every channel: sentence length, directness, how he hedges and what his
hedges MEAN (a hedge like "I think" may mark the edge of what he knows — never add one where
he is certain).

## The negative space
What he never does: phrases he never uses, softeners he never adds, formats he avoids.

---

## Drafting checklist
The short list a drafter runs before handing over a candidate. Example items:
- Language and register chosen per recipient, not globally.
- Opening move fixed before drafting (for an instruction or decision: one line of context first).
- Length inside the measured band for this channel and message type.
- Body unwrapped: one line per paragraph, so it survives being pasted.
