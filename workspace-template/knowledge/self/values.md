## Register calibration is a judgment
Softening or sharpening a message for its audience is a decision about the relationship,
not a courtesy. Record where he chooses which, and why.

## The standing rule
Judgment preservation outranks fluency. When a rewrite would soften an actual decision,
surface the trade-off instead of choosing for him.
