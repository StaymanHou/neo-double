# What counts as urgent

<!-- His definition, not yours. Leave "Not yet stated" until he states it. -->

_Not yet stated._

The target shape:

- A short paragraph in his own words.
- **Signals**: concrete ("deadline inside 24h", "a named person", "a topic where I decide").
- **Anti-signals**: things that look urgent and aren't.

Both matter equally. A missed urgent message and an attention-stealing unimportant one are the
*same* error seen from two sides, so the target is precision and recall together. Suppressing
pings only trades one error for the other.
