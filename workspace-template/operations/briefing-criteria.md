Two rules that hold before he states anything:

- **Check his outbound before calling anything open.** A request read without his reply is half
  a thread.
- **Never rank threads by volume when choosing what to read.** Volume measures activity, not
  consequence; his own outbound is the signal and is always a small fraction of the traffic.
